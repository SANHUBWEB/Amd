Step 1 — AMD Account Creation

To create an account, complete the AMD form below.

By creating an account, you agree to the AMD Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Step 2 — Activate Your Account

Please check your e-mail for your AMD account activation message.

Access Token and Password are sensitive credentials. Enter them directly on the official AMD page when requested; this tool does not store or transmit them.

Step 3 — Verify

Enter the verification code on the official AMD verification page.

Do not send OTP/verification codes to this tool or save them in Firebase.

Step 4 — AMD Arena

Open the AMD Arena information page and continue registration.

Open AMD Arena

Step 5 — AMD Arena Access Request

Complete the AMD Arena Access Request form.

Open AMD Arena Access Request

Pairing expires after 10 minutes. Review all filled AMD fields and submit the official AMD forms yourself.

Advanced — Import Excel / CSV

Import your prepared Excel file, review the rows, tick only the records you want, then send only the selected non-sensitive form data.

Supported columns: First Name, Last Name, E-mail, Preferred Language, Location, Company Name, Address 1, Address 2, State/Province, City, Postal Code, Phone, Job Function, Primary Role, Store ID, Marketing.

Do not include: passwords, access tokens, OTP/verification codes, payment details or government ID numbers.
